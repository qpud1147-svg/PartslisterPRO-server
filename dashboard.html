AI reply instructions

What the AI is told when you click AI draft on a message. Write it however you like. These rules are always added on top, whatever you write: use only facts from the listing, the order and the conversation; never make up dates, deadlines, fitment or policies; plain text, in English.

When a buyer has a problem

The AI works out which kind of problem the buyer has, then answers the way you pick here. For damaged, defective and wrong-part problems the return window applies — if it has ended, the AI says so politely. (The app works out the dates from the sale and your listing's return policy.)

The "Didn't arrive" choice above is for packages eBay doesn't show delivered yet. Either way, the draft starts with the carrier and tracking number, and if the buyer asks who received it, it says tracking only shows it was delivered, not who.

For TCMs, PCMs, ECMs, BCMs and other modules, the draft first asks whether it was programmed to their vehicle, then offers the return if it was and still doesn't work.

Photos and getting back to buyers

When a draft says you'll check something or get back to the buyer, it uses this for when (for example "I'll take a look Monday afternoon"). Leave it empty and drafts won't name a day. Sending a reply like that adds the conversation to Waiting on me in Messages until you mark it complete.

Tone and sign-off

The app adds your sign-off to the end of every draft, word for word. Leave it empty for none.

Fitment questions

When a buyer asks if a part fits, the AI says whether their vehicle is in the listing's fitment list (it never promises fit). If the listing has a part number, it asks them to compare it with the number on their original part and writes the number in the message. If there's no part number, it says so and asks them to compare their original part with the listing photos. Leave the VIN box off until you have a way to check VINs; when it's off, the AI never asks for one.

When a buyer asks for a discount

When it's on and the buyer asks for a lower price, the AI draft says that's the best you can do and that you've sent an offer. The offer is sent when you press Send (there's a checkbox to skip it), so a draft you throw away never sends a discount. eBay only lets apps send an offer to everyone watching the item or with it in their cart, so the draft also says "if you don't receive it, let me know". When it's off, the AI says the price is as listed.

Translate

Only messages that aren't already in English are translated, each one once. Every translated message has a See original link. Uses your Anthropic key.

Step 1

Register these addresses with eBay

In your eBay developer account → User Tokens → Add eBay Redirect URL, choose OAuth, and paste these in. They fill in automatically from wherever this app is running — if your web address ever changes, these change too, and you paste the new ones into eBay.

Step 2

Save the RuName eBay gives you

After you save on eBay, it shows a name for that entry, like Peter_Hoffmann-PeterHof-Lister-abcde. Copy it here.

Step 3

Connect

Opens eBay's sign-in. Approve access once and you're done — your sales import automatically after that.
