Yard tickets

Each yard’s ticket is a list of rows. Click a row to change its font, size (1–100), boldness (0–100) and position (every slider also has a number box), use the arrows to move it up or down, and add text, divider, logo or barcode rows. Use Duplicate to copy a whole ticket and change just a few things. Set a print time on a yard to put that time on its tickets (Randomize adds up to 30 minutes, never earlier); mark one text row "This row has the date" to say where the date goes. In a text row these fill in when you sell a ticket: {yard} {price} {number}, {date} and {time} (when it was sold) and {valid} (the day it’s good for). Fonts come from the computer that prints, so pick one that computer has.
