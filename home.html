Yard tickets

Each yard’s ticket is a list of rows. Click a row to change its font, size (1–100), bold and position, use the arrows to move it up or down, and add text, divider, logo or barcode rows. In a text row these fill in when you sell a ticket: {yard} {price} {number}, {date} and {time} (when it was sold) and {valid} (the day it’s good for). Fonts come from the computer that prints, so pick one that computer has.
