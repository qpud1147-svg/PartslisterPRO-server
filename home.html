Yard tickets

In the ticket text, these fill in when you sell a ticket: {yard} {price} {number}, {date} and {time} (when it was sold) and {valid} (the day it's good for). Put {logo} or {barcode} on a line of its own to place them there, and start a line with {big} to print it large. Start a line with {size=1} to {size=100} for any text size, or {left} / {center} / {right} to align that line. Without {logo}, the logo prints at the top; without any {big}, the first line prints large. A line of dashes prints as a divider. Fonts come from the computer that prints, so pick one that computer has.
